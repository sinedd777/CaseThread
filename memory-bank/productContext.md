# CaseThread Product Context

## Why This Project Exists

### Problem Statement
Legal professionals face significant challenges in research and document drafting:
- **Fragmented Research:** Legal research is scattered across multiple platforms and sessions
- **Memory Loss:** No persistent context between research sessions
- **Citation Errors:** Manual citation validation is time-consuming and error-prone
- **Inconsistent Quality:** Document drafting quality varies significantly
- **Jurisdictional Complexity:** Comparing legal doctrines across jurisdictions is difficult
- **Template Limitations:** Existing templates lack flexibility and intelligence

### Market Opportunity
- Legal technology market is rapidly growing
- Demand for AI-assisted legal research tools
- Need for intelligent document generation systems
- Gap in project-based legal memory systems

## Problems CaseThread Solves

### 1. Research Memory Persistence
- **Problem:** Research context is lost between sessions
- **Solution:** Project-scoped memory that retains queries, cases, highlights, and insights
- **Impact:** Continuity in legal research workflows

### 2. Multi-Domain Legal Reasoning
- **Problem:** Generic AI lacks specialized legal knowledge
- **Solution:** Domain-specific agents for different areas of law
- **Impact:** More accurate and contextually appropriate legal analysis

### 3. Citation Verification
- **Problem:** Manual citation checking is prone to errors
- **Solution:** Automated citation validation through CourtListener API
- **Impact:** Reliable, properly formatted legal citations

### 4. Document Quality Consistency
- **Problem:** Varying quality in legal document drafting
- **Solution:** Template-driven generation with multiple tone options
- **Impact:** Consistent, professional-quality legal documents

### 5. Cross-Jurisdiction Analysis
- **Problem:** Comparing laws across jurisdictions is complex
- **Solution:** Automated cross-jurisdiction comparison tools
- **Impact:** Strategic legal planning across multiple jurisdictions

## How It Should Work

### User Experience Flow
1. **Project Creation:** User creates a new legal project with jurisdiction and issue
2. **Research Phase:** 
   - Natural language queries are interpreted and structured
   - Multiple data sources are searched automatically
   - Results are ranked by relevance and authority
   - Key passages are highlighted and annotated
3. **Analysis Phase:**
   - Specialist agents provide domain-specific insights
   - Cross-jurisdiction comparisons are generated
   - Legal doctrines are mapped and compared
4. **Drafting Phase:**
   - User selects appropriate template and tone
   - Document is generated with embedded citations
   - Citations are automatically verified
   - Final document is exported in multiple formats

### Core User Interactions
- **Natural Language Queries:** "Find cases about Fourth Amendment vehicle searches"
- **Highlight Management:** Click-to-highlight important passages
- **Template Selection:** Choose from motion templates, briefs, contracts, etc.
- **Tone Adjustment:** Select assertive, conservative, or persuasive writing style
- **Citation Verification:** Automatic validation with visual indicators

## User Experience Goals

### Primary Users
- **Solo Practitioners:** Small firm lawyers needing research efficiency
- **Legal Researchers:** Paralegals and law clerks conducting case research
- **Law Students:** Academic research and writing assistance
- **Legal Departments:** In-house counsel managing multiple jurisdictions

- **Time Reduction:** 50% reduction in research time
- **Quality Improvement:** Consistent document quality across all outputs
- **Citation Accuracy:** 99%+ citation verification rate
- **User Satisfaction:** High adoption and retention rates
- **Workflow Integration:** Seamless integration with existing legal workflows
